Reservations, without picking up the phone.

THE PROBLEM

Every missed call is an empty table.

Phones ring at the worst time

Lunch rush, full house, hands covered in lime — that's exactly when reservation calls come in.

Same questions, all day

“¿A qué hora abren?” “¿Tienen ceviche mixto?” “¿Dónde quedan?” — answered a hundred times a week by a human.

No-shows and chaos

Reservations scribbled on paper, confirmations never sent, tables held for ghosts.

WHAT IT DOES

A host that never sleeps.

F—01

Instant reservations

Date, time, party size — confirmed in the chat in under a minute, with a confirmation the client can show at the door.

F—02

Menu answers

Knows the dishes, the prices, the recommendations. '¿Qué me recomiendas?' gets a real answer.

F—03

Hours & location

Opening hours, address, how to get there — answered before the client has to ask twice.

SEE IT WORK

A Friday night, handled.

No apps to install, no calls missed — the whole reservation happens in WhatsApp.
