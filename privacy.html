PRIVACY POLICY

Your data, handled plainly.

What we collect, why we collect it, and your rights. Last updated: October 2026.

Who we are

Vulpis is an AI sales-agent service for WhatsApp, built and operated by Snehil Sahay and Kenedy Garamendi (Lima, Peru × India). You can reach us at kennexhub@gmail.com for anything privacy-related.

What we collect

This website itself collects nothing about you — no accounts, no analytics, no tracking cookies. The only thing stored in your browser is your language preference.

When you message us on WhatsApp (for a free audit, a quote, or as a Vulpis client), we process what you send: your phone number, your display name, and the contents of your messages. Our AI agent reads these to qualify your request, answer questions, book meetings, and send payment links.

Why we use it

Only to run the service: reply to you, qualify leads, book meetings, and handle payments. We do not sell your data, and we do not use your conversations for advertising.

Who else touches your data

To deliver the service, messages pass through the infrastructure providers we use: WhatsApp / Meta (message delivery), Groq (AI replies), Supabase (secure storage), and Google Meet (scheduling). Each processes data only to provide their part of the service.

How long we keep it

Conversation records are kept while your project or client relationship is active, and for a reasonable period afterwards for support and accounting — then deleted on request.

Your rights

Write to kennexhub@gmail.com and we will show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. If you are a client's customer and reached our bot through their business line, you can also ask that business directly.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
