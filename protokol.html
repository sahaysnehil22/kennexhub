CASE STUDY — CONSTRUCTION TECH

Quality control, minus the paperwork.

PROTOKOL digitizes field quality-control protocols for the GORE Ayacucho highway works — an offline-first PWA that works in the mountains, signs per box, and generates PDF dossiers.

CLIENT

GORE Ayacucho — road project AY-728 / AY-729

STACK

TypeScript · SQLite · Supabase · PDF engine

STATUS

Live pilot in production

FIELD LEAD

Ing. David Valdez Ochoa — Quality Specialist

THE PROBLEM

Paper protocols die in the field.

Lost paperwork

QC protocols lived on loose sheets — rained on, misplaced, impossible to audit weeks later.

No traceability

Who signed what, when, where? On paper, that question has no reliable answer.

Zero connectivity

Mountain work sites have no signal. Any tool that needs the internet is a tool that doesn't work.

WHAT WE BUILT

A QC platform that works offline.

F—01

Offline-first PWA

Full functionality with zero connectivity. Drafts, photos and signatures queue locally and sync when signal returns.

F—02

Digital protocols

Topographic verification, steel reinforcement and more — the paper forms, rebuilt as structured digital checklists.

F—03

Photo evidence + GPS

Every protocol carries stamped photos with GPS provenance — proof of where and when it happened.

F—04

PDF dossiers

One-tap generation of the full protocol dossier — formatted to mirror the official paper structure.

F—05

Two-tier PIN auth

One company-controlled project PIN for access, personal PINs for signing. Per-box signatures, rate-limited and lockout-protected.

F—06

Cloud sync

Supabase-backed sync with idempotent submits — replay a submission and get the same result, never a duplicate.
